This directory includes compiled Kotlin standard library components (JetBrains and contributors)
and OpenCV 4.13.0 (OpenCV team and contributors). Their Apache 2.0 license is included separately.
OpenCV upstream source and third-party notices: https://github.com/opencv/opencv/tree/4.13.0
Official JavaScript release: https://docs.opencv.org/4.13.0/opencv.js
The JavaScript loader here has its embedded base64 WebAssembly payload separated into opencv-4.13.wasm.
